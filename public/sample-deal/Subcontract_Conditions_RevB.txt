SUBCONTRACT CONDITIONS — PACKAGE P2 BORED PILING
REVISION B — DRAFT FOR DISCUSSION

1. DEFINITIONS AND PRECEDENCE
1.1 "Contractor" means Contoh Bina Sdn Bhd. "Subcontractor" means the party named in the Letter of Award.
1.2 "Main Contract" means the contract between the Employer and the Contractor for the Project.
1.3 In the event of any conflict or discrepancy between the documents forming the Subcontract, the Contractor's interpretation shall prevail.

5. SCOPE OF WORKS
5.1 The Subcontractor shall carry out and complete the bored piling and empty bore works shown on the Contractor's drawings, including pile integrity and load testing.
5.2 The Subcontractor shall be deemed to have inspected the Site and satisfied itself as to the ground conditions, and no claim shall be allowed for any unforeseen ground conditions, obstructions or boulders.
5.4 The Contractor will provide access roads and a working platform. Removal of spoil off site is the responsibility of the Subcontractor.

9. VARIATIONS
9.1 No variation shall be valid unless instructed in writing and signed by a Director of the Contractor.
9.3 Any claim for a variation shall be submitted within fourteen (14) days of the instruction, failing which the Subcontractor shall be deemed to have waived its entitlement.
9.4 Variations shall be valued at the rates in the Bills of Quantities or, where no rates apply, at fair rates determined by the Contractor.

12. PAYMENT
12.1 The Subcontractor may submit a progress claim once every calendar month.
12.2 The Contractor shall assess and certify the claim within twenty-one (21) days of receipt.
12.3 Payment shall be made within sixty (60) days of certification, provided always that the Contractor shall not be obliged to pay any amount until and unless the Contractor has received payment of the corresponding amount from the Employer.
12.5 The Contractor may deduct or set off from any sum due to the Subcontractor any amount claimed by the Contractor under this Subcontract or any other contract between the parties.
12.7 Half of the retention shall be released upon the issue of the Certificate of Practical Completion for the Main Contract Works, and the balance upon the issue of the Certificate of Making Good Defects under the Main Contract.

14. TIME AND EXTENSION OF TIME
14.1 The Subcontractor shall execute the Works in accordance with the Contractor's master programme as updated from time to time.
14.2 The Subcontractor shall give written notice of any delay event within seven (7) days of its occurrence; such notice shall be a condition precedent to any extension of time.
14.3 The Subcontractor shall only be entitled to an extension of time to the extent that the Contractor is granted a corresponding extension of time by the Employer under the Main Contract.

15. LIQUIDATED DAMAGES
15.1 If the Subcontractor fails to complete the Works by the Completion Date, it shall pay the Contractor liquidated and ascertained damages at the rate stated in the Letter of Award for every day of delay.
15.2 In addition, the Subcontractor shall indemnify the Contractor against any liquidated damages imposed on the Contractor by the Employer to the extent caused by the Subcontractor.

16. DEFECTS
16.1 The Defects Liability Period shall be twenty-four (24) months from practical completion of the Main Contract Works.

18. TERMINATION
18.1 The Contractor may terminate the Subcontractor's employment at any time for its convenience by giving seven (7) days' notice, in which event the Subcontractor shall be paid the value of work properly executed and shall have no claim for loss of profit.
18.3 If the Subcontractor fails to proceed regularly and diligently, the Contractor may give notice to remedy within seven (7) days, failing which the Contractor may terminate.

19. SUSPENSION
19.1 The Subcontractor shall not suspend or slow down the Works for any reason whatsoever, including non-payment.

21. DISPUTES
21.1 Any dispute shall be referred to arbitration in Kuala Lumpur under the AIAC Arbitration Rules after completion of the Main Contract Works.
21.2 Nothing in this clause shall prevent either party from referring a payment dispute to adjudication under the Construction Industry Payment and Adjudication Act 2012.

22. INSURANCE
22.1 The Contractor shall maintain a Contractor's All Risks policy covering the Subcontract Works. The Subcontractor shall bear the deductible of RM 50,000 for each claim arising from its works.

23. INTEREST
23.1 Any amount certified but unpaid after the due date shall attract interest at five percent (5%) per annum.
